# Launch and growth plan

## Publishing checklist (App Store Connect)
1. Apple Developer Program as an Organization (needs a D-U-N-S number; can take weeks).
2. Archive in Xcode, upload, TestFlight internal then external beta (50-200 real drivers).
3. Privacy: App Privacy label must match `MilesProSync/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy` and the privacy policy;
   clear "Always" location purpose strings; review notes explaining background location + demo account.
4. Subscriptions: products matching `MilesProSync.storekit`, restore-purchases button, clear pricing,
   links to privacy policy and terms.
5. Listing: five benefit-led screenshots, 15-30s preview video, phased release with manual release date.

## Growth levers (priority order)
1. ASO + Apple Search Ads on generic and competitor terms; Product Page Optimization tests.
2. Ratings prompt after the first tax-ready export; target 4.7+ and 500+ ratings in quarter one.
3. SEO content: IRS mileage rate, mileage log template, deduction guides for rideshare.
4. Partnerships: CPAs/bookkeepers, freelancer and gig-driver communities, small-business newsletters.
5. Seasonal pushes: January, March-April, Q4.
6. Referrals: free month for both sides; Team invites as a viral loop.
7. Retention: monthly miles/deduction summary.

## Metrics
Download-to-trial and trial-to-paid conversion; day-7/day-30 retention; cost per paying subscriber by
channel; share of trips auto-classified.

## Before launch
Counsel review of privacy policy and subscription terms; careful tax-benefit wording; trademark check on
"Miles Pro Sync".

## Next steps
1. Capture top-10 App Store results for "mileage tracker" and "mileage log" (ratings, subtitles, screenshots).
2. Read MileIQ/Everlance 1-3 star reviews on the App Store itself.
3. Small Apple Search Ads test for per-keyword conversion data.
4. Decide free tier and pricing before the listing goes live.
