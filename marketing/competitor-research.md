# Competitor research

Last checked: 2026-11-03 via web search. Figures come from third-party roundups and vendor-authored
pages, and **disagree with each other**. Verify on each app's own pricing page and App Store listing.
Not yet gathered: App Store rankings, ratings counts, keyword popularity.

| App | Free tier | Paid price (conflicting) | Common complaints |
|---|---|---|---|
| MileIQ | 40 drives/month | ~$6-$9/month, ~$60-$90/year | Missed/merged/false trips; free cap used up by commuting; price increases |
| Everlance | 30 trips/month (one source: 40) | ~$8-$12/month | Free cap; broader expense scope adds bulk |
| TripLog | Reported generous; sources disagree | ~$5.99/month | Mixed reports on free limits |
| Stride | Free, no trip limits | None | Weak ratings (~2.8-3.1 in one source); more manual start/stop, missed miles |
| Hurdlr | Basic free | ~$10/month | Mileage bundled with income/tax features |
| Gridwise | Basic free | ~$5-$8/month | Aimed at gig drivers |

MileIQ is the category leader: ~4.5 Trustpilot (~1,500 reviews), 4.6 Google Play. Weak points are price
and the 40-drive cap.
