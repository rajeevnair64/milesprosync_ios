# Positioning and ASO

## Where Miles Pro Sync can win
1. Drive cap: commuters exhaust a 40-drive free limit. Decide a more generous free tier (open decision).
2. Classification effort: competitors need a swipe per drive; repeat routes here classify themselves.
3. Price: undercut ~$60-$90/year if the economics work (open decision).
4. Trust: be specific about on-device storage and what syncs (see PRIVACY.md).
5. Stride's gap: free but poorly rated; room for free-to-start with reliable auto tracking.

Adjust the last clause to the final free-tier decision.
